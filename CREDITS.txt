Original mod: Toshiro Hitsugaya (Bleach), Workshop 736626219
Original author field: Silhh and Arcade
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=736626219

This restoration started from the user's subscribed version 1.1.1.
Original animation rigs, timings, asset identifiers and source assets derive
from that package. The legacy dialogue was replaced as described below.
The original mod credits Ysovuka/Kzisor for its transformation framework and
Shamantitan for the freeze spell. The old loader, UI patches and keyhandler are
not included in this compatibility version.

This restoration does not claim ownership of the original work or itself grant
permission to redistribute its assets. No original-author redistribution
authorization is asserted here. BLEACH characters and setting belong to their
respective rights holders. This is an unofficial fan mod, unaffiliated with
Klei or the official BLEACH production.

Combat extension 1.2.0-combat.1 originally introduced hitsugaya_swordwave_fx,
procedural ice-crescent artwork generated by this project (12 frames, 30 fps).
It does not copy game textures. The generator and editable atlas are outside the
mod package. Current effect textures were subsequently redrawn in the art revisions.

Art working version 1.2.0-art.1: character, ghost, equipment, effects and UI images
were redrawn with the built-in ImageGen tool from the user-approved V1 concept.
The source PNGs and prompts are kept in docs/art/production outside this package.
The original animation timing/rig contract is retained; textures and build UVs
are repacked. This historical version had proportion and facial-expression issues;
the art.2 corrections below supersede it, without claiming full visual acceptance.

Art correction 1.2.0-art.2: atlas cells are split along transparent gutters to
retain the full chin, blade and ice parts. Neutral face frames share registered
artwork/geometry, eliminating independently redrawn neutral-eye jumps. Distinct
closed-eye expressions and original animation timing are retained. This removes
subtle differences between the original neutral/open-eye transitional frames.
The source correction and checks remain outside the runtime package. Full client
visual acceptance across all actions/equipment is still pending.

Gameplay development build 1.3.0-gameplay.1 added the user-requested sanity economy,
cold affinity, bounded in-place sword soul strengthening through native crafting,
and a six-slot haori. Five native crafting recipes strengthen the existing sword
in place, including two alternatives for stage III. No new inventory item prefab
is added; the character, sword and haori identifiers and art.2 media are retained.

Version 1.3.1 repairs new-character haori delivery and the empty equipment-slot
icon, corrects Melonsicle recognition, and supplies the default skin build mapping.
It revises character/equipment/recipe text and adds 247 original Chinese/English
dialogue pairs written for this version. Remaining speech contexts fall back to
the current native character speech table, rather than the legacy Wilson template.
Chinese locales use Chinese; other locales use English. Player chat is not
translated. Existing damage, upgrade materials and progression caps are unchanged.

Validation recorded on 2026-11-01, against local DST build 761239:
- The 75-case regression suite passed: 73 cases ran in the native dedicated
  engine; 2 cases use simulated UI/client closures within that suite.
- 42 offline gameplay and 18 offline localization contract checks passed.
- A real local English client verified haori pickup/equipping, six-slot storage,
  removal/re-equipping with contents retained, wooden-armor coexistence, visual
  priority, and right-click container open/close.
These are distinct validation scopes, not 75 real multiplayer acceptance tests.
Mixed-language play on two real clients, controllers, cross-cave migration,
complete crafting-UI flow, all equipment/action animations and overall balance
have not all completed acceptance. Offline previews are not gameplay recordings.
Full validation details and historical results remain in the project's
docs/compatibility-release.md, outside this runtime package.
